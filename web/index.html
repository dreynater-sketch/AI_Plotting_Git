How to use it

  • Click anything to pick it. Change it on the right.
  • Drag it to move it.
  • Double-click words to type new ones.
  • Hold Ctrl and click to pick more than one.
  • Drag across an empty spot to pick everything inside.
  • Add things from the Insert menu.
